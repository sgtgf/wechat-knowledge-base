**摘 要**

  

  

现代/分布式电力能源系统中，可再生能源（光伏、风能、波能和水力）的渗透率不断提高，其输出功率也呈现出时变特性，因此需要更强/更高频率的交流（AC）（直流（DC））电压控制。实际上，当存在频率和电压波动时，需要更快且更稳定的有功和无功功率。由电力电子控制的变速发电机没有足够的能量存储（惯性）来应对这一范围（包括静态同步补偿器（STATCOM））。这是因为电力电子技术往往会将发电机与电力系统解耦。虽然双馈感应发电机（DFIG）中的虚拟惯性控制为这些问题提供了一种部分解决方案，但需要一个更强大且更全面的框架来实现高级电网支持。通过扩展双励磁原理，双轴励磁电动同步发电机（DE-SG）提供两种变体的卓越灵活性，总结如下：作为一种多功能的双馈感应发电机（DFIG）与双轴与单轴励磁的同步发电机（SG），以及采用双直流和交流励磁（作为带有惯性轮的空载DFIG）的同步调相机（SC），通过采用变速方式加速/减速SC，为频率稳定提供额外的辅助作用。这些解决方案适用于短时间的瞬态情况，但并不旨在取代大型双向储能系统（水泵、氢气、电池等），这些系统对于具有多个电源的现代电力系统中输出能量的日常固有变化至关重要。本文概述了双轴激励与单轴激励的同步发电机（SE-SGs）以及SC拓扑、建模和控制，以提高现代多源能源系统的稳定性。该调查包括多个案例研究，以阐明突出的方法。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

Modern/distributed electric energy systems, with ever larger penetration of renewable (photovoltaic, wind, wave, and hydro) energy sources and time-variable outputs, are in need of stronger/higher frequency and alternating current (AC) (direct current (DC)) voltage control. In fact, faster and more stable active and reactive power in the presence of frequency and voltage sags and swells is needed. Power electronics-controlled variable speed generators do not have enough energy storage (inertia) for the scope (static synchronous compensators (STATCOMs) included). This is because power electronics tends to decouple the generator from the power system. While virtual inertia control in doubly fed induction generators (DFIGs) offers a partial solution to these problems, a more robust and comprehensive framework is required for advanced grid support. This is how, by extending the dual-excitation principles, the dual-axis excited electric synchronous generators (DE-SG) provide superior flexibility in two variants summarized here: as a multifunctional DFIG and dual-axis vs. single-axis excited synchronous generator (SG), and as a synchronous condenser (SC), with dual DC and AC excitation (as a no-load DFIG with inertia wheel), where variable speed is used to accelerate/ decelerate the SC and thus provide additional assistance in frequency stabilization. These solutions, good for short-time transients, are not meant, however, to replace the large bidirectional energy storage systems (pump-hydro, hydrogen, batteries, etc.) which are crucial for the daily inherent variations of output energy in modern power systems with multiple power sources. The present paper offers a summary of techniques used in the dual-axis excited vs. single-axis excited SGs (SE-SGs), and SCs topologies, modeling, and control for better stability in modern multiple-source energy systems. This survey includes multiple case studies to shed light on prominent methods.
