# 到底是“浮夸宣传”，还是“获奖级工作”？


> 原文地址: [https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/aScDtKvYcx3Y3Y0eRWgklg](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/aScDtKvYcx3Y3Y0eRWgklg)

在“[秋水文章不染尘：本团队青年学者才思迸发，一举攻克图像配准50年难题](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s?__biz=Mzk2NDk3NjgzOA==&mid=2247488748&idx=1&sn=b2b89f566920101af29795f2321c266c&scene=21#wechat_redirect)”一文中，我们介绍了朱亮亮博士在图像配准相位噪声抑制方面的工作。文章发出后，引起了一些读者的兴趣，也招致了部分读者的质疑，甚至贴上“浮夸宣传”的标签。

为回应关切，我们整理并公开部分审稿专家的评审意见，以供读者参考。相关文章于2019年投稿TGRS，经一轮修改后于2020年被接受。共有四位评审专家参与评审，接下来我们呈现其中一位专家在两轮审稿中的核心意见。

Comments to the Author（First Round）  
The normalized cross-power spectrum based methods easily affected by noise and 2-D phase extraction. To eliminate the influence of noise and avoid the problem of 2-D phase extraction problem, this manuscript proposed the concept of auto-correlated normalized cross-power spectrum. The detailed experimental results illustrate the effectiveness of this concept.  The idea is very interesting and the organization of the paper is good.

Comments to the Author（Second Round）The changes have very much improved the paper, even though the original submission was of high quality by itself.  I find that the comparisons among many methods for different types of noise are highly illuminating.  The treatment with many different forms of noise increases the utility of the results, and confirms the power of the method.  The comparison among the spectral bands in Fig. 12 of the revision is especially important because it reveals the precision of the various methods in a way that was absent in the original paper.  For example, Fig. 12(a) and Fig 12(f) show substantial differences in the precision of the methods, and Fig. 12(c) suggests the method is off by a pixel for some bands.  Because of the thoroughness of the study and the information that it has developed regarding the relative qualities of the methods, I have suggested the revised paper be considered for a prize.  

I have no suggestions for further revisions, and accept the revised paper as submitted.

从上述意见可以清晰地看到，该专家在首轮评审中即敏锐地指出，论文提出的“自相关归一化互功率谱”概念，精准攻克了传统方法易受噪声干扰及二维相位提取的两大痛点，直言“研究思路极具新意”。

修订稿评审更是给出高度评价，不仅认可“修改后的论文质量大幅提升（尽管初稿已属上乘）”，还盛赞其对多种噪声类型的系统性对比“极具启发意义”。特别是新增的波段间对比实验，被评价为“填补了初稿空白，精准揭示了各方法的精度差异”。最终，评审因“研究的彻底性与高质量”，**明确提议该论文应该参与奖项评选（Prize Paper）。**

**那么，这项工作到底是“浮夸宣传”，还是“获奖级成果”？**

**真理越辩越明，我们期待并欢迎各位读者的理性探讨。**
