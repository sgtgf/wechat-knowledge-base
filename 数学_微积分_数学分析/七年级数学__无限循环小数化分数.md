> 原文地址: [https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/47l9bOHv8\_AFGvAmWyIdPQ](https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/47l9bOHv8_AFGvAmWyIdPQ)

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntI9sP7wAp5piahiaM2M5SicMnlib2b8kpmvsTlX2icMnAjE799dW0gN6kEVVg/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

图片标题是\*\*“无限循环小数化分数”\*\*，核心在讲一件事：

> **无限循环小数（有循环节的那种）一定能写成分数（有理数）**，而且写法有固定套路：  
> **“乘一个 10 的幂让循环对齐 → 相减把循环消掉 → 解方程”。**

* * *

## 1）先看图里的第一个例子：![$0.\dot 7$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIJ7T7bEjAOiauic3pluPayNOZ8kgDoiaA7sBib0xicJKPfVB2rgk1GcRoGSA/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

图中做法是：

设

![$x = 0.\dot 7 = 0.7777\ldots$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntITS4ibRvck9X6LpjUvVd3mEibX1sFYSwibZOehTMCAfC52Qy4wicR95A7PQ/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

循环节只有 **1 位**，所以乘 10：

![$10x = 7.7777\ldots$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntI86cmHXGgKAO5DSEC0TibKiaOopw3X8JljC22vx8GmmgYfLgmLRG0GO0w/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

两式相减，让无限重复部分“抵消”：

![$10x-x = 7.7777\ldots-0.7777\ldots = 7$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIrBjibdyM7Nw7oKNBK51sGuC9T8ibmNibplMCGicut9icpkKs4nfMXMBDVibA/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

![$9x=7 \Rightarrow x=\frac79$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIVFVDsMzRgvnsqB5foh2FY5viahYsY9B2lkBY8hx4AbeKbIV8e3t94vg/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

所以

![$0.\dot7=\frac79$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIg6EqoLXQVZntZu6aVhaZFKmFzgr59fckHTCMPMVXkkIDw6Jfia0VCgg/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

顺便你也能立刻得到：

![$0.\dot1=\frac19,\;0.\dot2=\frac29,\;\ldots,\;0.\dot9=\frac99=1$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIJ9wjob79Hc4lDrl4AxOPa3uvkwD1fsicibdIh73ghHILH5ukic4HI4Y6g/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

## 3）把方法总结成“通用规律”

### A. 纯循环小数（小数点后立刻开始循环）

如果循环节是 n 位，比如

![$x = 0.\overline{a_1a_2\ldots a_n}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntI4svPibpYvsibCP3mUgkQ7EagJAV5LeerxbiajLJfPqnHiaXwwa8zbXz12g/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

那么直接记住结论：

![$x=\frac{a_1a_2\ldots a_n}{\underbrace{99\ldots 9}_{n\text{ }9}}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntItna95dRJ8SuMpiaWLwicZeSsryXG8IqwyHskgY4JDaicLggbygcfKu63g/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

-   1 位循环：分母 9
    
-   2 位循环：分母 99
    
-   3 位循环：分母 999
    
-   …
    

例如图里最后让你试的：

-   ![$0.\dot{735}=0.735735\ldots$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIBZoic9t8ialNQmdvZZSGxkt2QaLI8f8VmiclYNokNeADcjqdltGtkkDgQ/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)
    

![$0.\dot{735}=\frac{735}{999}=\frac{245}{333}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIxKl92dBUpwkxCc0TtYUqmiaKyDH4IUz4TsZyJKz6k92MoRwBtRRiaA2A/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

-   ![$0.\dot{8231}=0.82318231\ldots$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIpvDj6YokYRtJA5Ga0eibY3LfgNic2pokHjHazAQGRicEPqItyKItiaUp4A/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)
    

![$0.\dot{8231}=\frac{8231}{9999}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIY2PkJjKD9WghVdFEVfmWgmUhQgjnjcQYTeLvDSQRlmZIkiaIkZ5SCyw/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

### B. 混循环小数（前面有一段不循环，后面才循环）

比如：

![$x = 0.\underbrace{(\text{no repeating }m\text{ bit})}_{A}\underbrace{(\text{repeating }n\text{ bit})}_{B}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIC8exunLJGXrLJJGjesmqn0OicqMXicJZwp58hNkyMqxp3RE3QA5RkCYA/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

最稳的“公式版”是：

> **把“小数点后：不循环+循环”拼成一个整数 N1**  
> **把“小数点后：只保留不循环”拼成一个整数 N0**  
> 则

![$x=\frac{N_1-N_0}{10^m(10^n-1)}$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIKrYWBFMlBEeevATCKv8oFYlibdzf1iaGhY7nourEyI1j32Saia7gFlsmw/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)

你可以把它理解成：

-   乘 ![$10^m$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIs98PdOA0cAxwfN0wV6yibzztUPGbl85dVicLyicOXmXmp6NtdMsw4FN6w/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)：把不循环部分移到小数点左边
    
-   再乘 ![$10^n$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntIGG9LGR3pTuvkP5jVj6Fiavxg6VtoUwtds5zl95HF5icW68vWB5GkF6rA/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)：让循环对齐
    
-   相减：把循环消掉
    
-   分母就自然变成 ![$10^m(10^n-1)$](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jlXjovro0trKRPCNoZEyBEJ0R9O1ibntI46ctHA8BRPvnticg7COV38eTGT7FQq18dfmQMYsKjaAl0QTugZp59xg/640?wx_fmt=png&from=appmsg)
