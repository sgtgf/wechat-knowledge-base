**摘 要**

  

  

对于混合动力电动无人机（UAV），机载永磁同步发电机（PMSG）的稳定供电至关重要。密闭舱内过热会直接导致电源中断和系统故障。因此，积极改进热管理不仅是确保飞行可靠性和任务成功的关键技术前提，而且可以提高机器的效率和系统的整体功率密度。针对无人机应用中的严格空间约束，研究并强调了紧凑型外转子发电机转子侧壁上的新型自空冷散热拓扑。开发了一个以多目标遗传算法为中心的系统优化框架，以帕累托优化确定翅片几何形状，从而平衡热性能和气动损失。所提出的拓扑结构创新地部署在转子侧壁上，将外转子机器的结构空间与自空气冷却独特地结合在一起，产生不同模式的定向气流，直接提高定子的冷却效率。通过多目标遗传算法对所设计的自风冷散热拓扑结构的参数进行了优化。无风条件下的温升试验表明，所提出的自空冷结构在5000 r/min下将发电机的定子温度降低了37.1°C，证实了实际应用的有效性和工程可行性。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

For hybrid-electric unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), the stable power supply from the onboard permanent magnet synchronous generator (PMSG) is critical. Overheating in the confined compartment can directly lead to power interruption and system failure. Therefore, proactively improving the thermal management is not only a key technical prerequisite for ensuring flight reliability and mission success, but also enhances the machine's efficiency and the overall power density of the system. Targeting the stringent spatial constraints in UAV applications, novel self-air-cooling heat dissipation topologies are investigated and highlighted on the rotor sidewall for compact outer-rotor generators. A systematic optimization framework, centered on a multi-objective genetic algorithm, is developed to Pareto-optimize the fin geometries, balancing thermal performance against aerodynamic penalty. The proposed topologies are innovatively deployed on the rotor sidewall, uniquely combining the structural space of an outer-rotor machine with self-air-cooling to generate directed airflow of varying patterns that directly enhance the cooling efficiency of the stator. The parameters of the designed self-air-cooled heat dissipation topologies are optimized via a multi-objective genetic algorithm. A temperature rise test under windless conditions shows that the proposed self-air-cooled structure reduces the stator temperature of the generator by 37.1 °C at 5000 r/min, confirming the effectiveness and engineering feasibility for practical applications.
