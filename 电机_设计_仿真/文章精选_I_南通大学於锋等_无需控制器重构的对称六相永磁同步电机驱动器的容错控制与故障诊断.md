**摘 要**

  

  

由于其多自由度特性和固有的容错能力，六相电机在电动汽车推进和航空航天系统等大功率应用中得到了广泛的应用。本文提出了在孤立中性点拓扑下对称六相永磁同步电机（SSPMSM）的容错控制策略，并提出了一种基于多变量联合诊断的故障诊断方案。首先，建立了SSPMSM的两个数学模型及其关系。随后，分析了故障条件下两个绕组组的转矩子空间和谐波子空间中的电流矢量，并将故障后转矩脉动的原因解释为控制器冲突造成的。此外，提出了一种基于x−y子空间电压阈值和α−β子空间电流轨迹特征的多元故障诊断方案，以提高诊断精度。最后，通过实验验证了所提出的控制和诊断方法的可行性和稳定性。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

Owing to the multi-degree-of-freedom characteristics and inherent fault-tolerant capacity, six-phase motors have been widely adopted in high-power applications, such as electric vehicle propulsion and aerospace systems. This paper presents the fault-tolerant control strategy of symmetrical six-phase permanent magnet synchronous motor (SSPMSM) under an isolated neutral point topology and proposes a fault diagnosis scheme based on joint diagnosis of multiple variables. First, two mathematical models of SSPMSM and their relationship are established. Subsequently, the current vectors in the torque subspace and harmonic subspace of the two winding sets under fault conditions are analyzed, and the cause of post-fault torque ripple is explained as resulting from controller conflict. In addition, a multivariate fault diagnosis scheme based on voltage threshold in the x−y subspace and current trajectory characteristics in the α−β subspace is proposed to enhance the diagnostic accuracy. Finally, the feasibility and stability of the proposed control and diagnosis methods are verified by experiments.
