**摘 要**

  

  

本文提出了一种提高电动机功率因数和转矩的协同设计方法。首先，探讨了永磁体（PM）和电枢磁场的磁链分析与功率因数之间的内在关系。然后，建立了磁链与谐波之间的联系，阐明了提高功率因数和转矩的机理。改进的重点是PM和渗透率。关于永磁结构，采用Y形永磁结构有效地提高了永磁利用率，减少了外端的漏磁通，增强了永磁磁链。关于磁导，定子齿设计经过优化，以协同提高磁导谐波，减少电枢磁场的非工作磁链，并增强负责扭矩产生的电枢磁场的基波调制波。这在保持电机扭矩的同时提高了功率因数。最后，通过永磁结构设计，优化了电机转矩性能。此外，还对Y型永磁电机的性能进行了评估。制造并测试了一个原型。理论分析和实验结果在很大程度上证明了所提出方法的有效性。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

This paper proposes a collaborative design method for enhancing the power factor and torque of electric motors. First, the intrinsic relationship between flux linkage analysis of the permanent magnet (PM) and armature field and the power factor is explored. Then, the connection between flux linkage and harmonics is established, clarifying the mechanism for improving power factor and torque. Improvements are focused on the PM and permeance. Regarding the PM structure, employing a Y-shaped PM structure effectively increases PM utilization, reduces leakage flux at the outer ends, and enhances the PM flux linkage. Concerning permeance, stator tooth design is optimized to cooperatively improve permeance harmonics, reduce the non-working flux linkage of the armature field, and enhance the fundamental modulation wave of the armature field responsible for torque generation. This improves the power factor while maintaining motor torque. Finally, through PM structural design, the motor torque performance is optimized. Furthermore, the performance of the Y-shaped PM motor is evaluated. A prototype was manufactured and tested. Theoretical analysis and experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed method to a significant extent.
