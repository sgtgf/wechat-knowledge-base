**基于鲁棒二分法以及扰动观测器的并网逆变器模型预测控制**

**Robust Dichotomy Solution-Based Model Predictive Control for the Grid-Connected Inverters with Disturbance Observer**

**Authors:** **Huanyue Liao, Xin Zhang, Zhijun Ma**

**DOI:10.30941/CESTEMS.2021.00011**

**https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9473186**

  

**Abstract**

     

 This paper proposes a robust dichotomy-based model predictive control (DS-MPC) with a fixed switching frequency for the grid-connected inverter (GCI). The proposed fast dichotomy algorithm can select and deduce the optimal voltage vector dynamically through the space vector plane. Therefore, the proposed DS-MPC strategy could ensure dynamic performance and steady-state performance as well. Also, the current control robustness can be improved through DS-MPC with disturbance observer (DO) based on the extended Kalman filter (EKF). The novelty of this control is that the current control with a fast dynamic response can be realized in the weak grid, even if the grid voltages are greatly distorted. Simulation and hardware experiments on the weak grid validate the effectiveness of the proposed DS-MPC with the EKF observer approach.

  

**Methodology**

  

 The topology and control strategy of the GCI is becoming significant as the increasing use in electrical systems and several interviews have been reported focusing on this field. The proposed DS-MPC strategy with the voltage observer based on EKF is deduced for the three-phase inverter. Noted that the three-phase currents and voltages are transferred to the rectangular coordinate system. Then through the model predictive equations, the predicted currents are deduced. The observed grid voltage is calculated by the EKF observer. The details of the EKF observer are explained below. Besides, the cost functions based on the dichotomy solution are conveyed to the PWM modulator, then the optimal voltage vector can be determined in comparison with the reference current. Therefore, it is quasi-continuous for the selected optimal voltage vector. Finally receives the switching state of each inverter is collected and the currents are well-regulated as anticipation.    

   The cost function in current control is determined according to the absolute error compared with the predicted current and the reference, which can be manifested as

![](https://mmbiz.qpic.cn/mmbiz_png/jFLbUGmZ178bshX9EHBcNIFgWibDj4VlH3d7lFvRfxGgthz1icCYpnZxPf6uNu7AdfA4X8vkm0OjPo7vzzwfnuibg/640?wx_fmt=png)

where gI is added for the overcurrent protection.

   To realize the function optimization algorithm, the dichotomy is applied in the control strategy. The search area for the voltage vector is shown in Fig. 1, which is determined by a circular plane with the radius as the maximum available voltage Um Hence, through the internal circle of the hexagon, which is integrated by the active voltage vectors, the circle plane is deduced. As the amplitude Um/2 and phase π/4 are different in every switching period, the eight voltage vectors are chosen at the initialization. Then, one of the eight vectors is selected according to the cost function as the optimal vector in the initial step of the iteration. Therefore, with the updated amplitude and phase angles by half of the final step optimal vector, there are 18 new vectors are selected in the final step to calculate the cost function. The search area narrows to half of the last step. The optimal vector is chosen after 14 steps iteration based on the reference vector for the PWM modulator.

   According to the characteristics of the weak grid, the grid has a large inductive impedance, and the grid voltage is heavily affected by harmonics. Due to MPC is designed through the equivalent model of the three-phase GCI, the distorted voltage cannot be considered. Therefore, the performance can be degraded with conventional MPC.

   The EKF is a recursive predictor-corrector type estimator. To demonstrate the EKF algorithm, its algorithm flow chart is presented in Fig. 2. It can be seen that EKF mainly includes two steps of iteration. The first step is called filtering. The state of the system is transitioned forward using the plant model, and at the same time, a Kalman gain is calculated. Then the forwarded state is corrected based on the Kalman gain and the measurement error. Simultaneously, the error covariance matrix is updated as well. The inputs of the system are the initial state and the initial error covariance matrix.

**Experimental Results**

  

The control strategy is utilized Through the FPGA-based controller. Then according to CORDIC (Coordinate Rotation Digital Computer) algorithm, complex trigonometric and quadratic functions are computed. Both of the conventional FS-MPC, the traditional linear strategies, and the proposed DS-MPC are verified and compared through the experimental testbench.

   The experimental results indicated that all control strategies can achieve satisfied dynamic performance. The results clearly demonstrate the high-quality dynamic performance of the proposed DS-MPC method as, unlike those for the closed-loop PI control scheme, less ripple is seen after each stepping. The current THD (total harmonics distortion) of the proposed DS-MPC is 2.74%, which is much less than 4.12% for conventional FS-MPC and 3.26% for the linear method. Moreover, the control of DS-MPC is the simpler design of the filter to diminish the harmonic for the harmonics regularly spread around the switching frequency, compared with the traditional FS-MPC which more current harmonics existing. This can be explained by the low bandwidth nature of the main PI-based current controllers. Since the grid interface is an isolation transformer, the grid is fundamentally a weak grid and the grid voltage is to some extent also distorted by the filter resonance. Upon the activation of the cost function, the resonance can be suppressed.

Therefore, the proposed robust DS-MPC strategy can ensure the dynamic performance of the current control for the GCI in the weak grid.

  

**Conclusion**

  

A robust DS-MPC with an EKF observer for grid voltage has been proposed for the three-phase inverter system with the ouput _L_ filter.

With the voltage vector dichotomy solution method, the ideal voltage vector could be dynamically chosen in a short period through the space vector plane. Therefore, the PWM modulator can be combined based on the output voltage vector with quasi-continuous characteristics. Because the THD spread in a specific range with the fixed switching frequency, the design of the GCIs’ output current filter can be simplified than the traditional FS-MPC. Moreover, the proposed control method shows fast convergence and high robustness to grid voltage disturbance. Based on the well-designed and turned EKF observer, the grid voltage can be estimated precisely. The control algorithm is implemented based on an FPGA controller through hardware optimization methods such as pipeline and resource sharing. The high-performance current control with the weak grid can be realized with the proposed control method. The simulation and experimental results under the weak grid case based on the real prototype verified the effectiveness of the proposed method.
