**摘 要**

  

  

本文比较了使用铜（Cu）和铝（AI）绕组的电励磁同步电机（EESM）在电动汽车（EV）应用中用铝绕组代替铜绕组的可行性，因为铝绕组的质量密度和单位重量成本较低，但电阻率较高，导热率较低。采用电磁-热联合优化方法对四种绕组结构的EESMs进行了优化设计。本研究将仅使用铜绕组的优化EESM视为基准。结果表明，定子铜/转子铝绕组的EESM的扭矩降低最小（12.1%），且在所有EESM中扭矩质量密度最高。同时，虽然定子铜/转子铝EESM的新欧洲驱动循环效率比基准值低1.8%，但单位成本的扭矩高71%，最大转子机械应力降低8%。因此，对于电动汽车应用而言，考虑到性能和成本之间的权衡，具有定子铜/转子铝绕组的EESM有望替代仅采用铜绕组的 EESM。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

In this paper, electrically excited synchronous machines (EESMs) using copper (Cu) and aluminum (AI) windings are compared for the feasibility of replacing Cu windings with Al windings in electric vehicle (EV) applications since Al windings have lower mass density and cost per weight, but higher resistivity and lower thermal conductivity than Cu windings. The EESMs with four winding configurations are optimized with an electromagnetic-thermal co-optimization method. The optimized EESM with only Cu windings is considered as the baseline in this study. Results show that the EESM with stator-Cu/rotor-Al windings has the least torque reduction (12.1%) compared to the baseline among the three EESMs with Al windings and the highest torque mass density among all EESMs. Meanwhile, although the new European driving cycle efficiency of the stator-Cu/rotor-Al EESM is 1.8% lower than that of the baseline, the torque per cost is 71% higher, and the maximum rotor mechanical stress is 8% lower. Therefore, the EESMs with stator-Cu/rotor-Al windings are prospective substitutions of those with only Cu windings for EV applications considering the trade-off between performance and cost.
