**摘 要**

  

  

分数槽集中绕组（FSCWs）广泛应用于六相容错永磁同步电机（PMSM）中。然而，FSCWs会导致定子磁动势（MMF）的大量空间谐波，并增加机器的损耗。为了抑制六相永磁同步电机的空间谐波，本文将六相绕组视为两组三相绕组的组合，并研究了两组三相线圈之间的移相对磁动势谐波抑制的影响。为了更有效地抑制MMF谐波，低空间谐波定向设计进一步扩展到双六相绕组之间的相移，同时相位数不加倍。设计了一种低空间谐波双六相15°移相绕组，可抑制单极对和五极对的磁动势谐波，并对其损耗、永磁体（PM）转矩和磁阻转矩特性进行了评估。研制了24槽/14极双六相15°移相永磁同步电机样机，并进行了实验验证。  

  

**Abstract**

  

  

Fractional-slot concentrated windings (FSCWs) are widely utilized in six-phase fault-tolerant permanent-magnet synchronous machines (PMSMs). However, FSCWs cause abundant space harmonics of stator magnetomotive force (MMF) and increase the losses of the machine. In this article, in order to suppress the space harmonics of a six-phase PMSM, the six-phase winding is regarded as a combination of two sets of three-phase windings, and the effect of phase-shifting between two sets of three-phase windings on the MMF harmonic suppression is investigated. To achieve more effective suppression of the MMF harmonics, the low-space-harmonic oriented design is further extended to phase-shifting between double six-phase windings while the phase number is not doubled. A low-space-harmonic double-six-phase 15°-shifting winding is designed, in which both 1-pole-pair and 5-pole-pair MMF harmonics are suppressed, and its characteristics of losses, permanent-magnet (PM) torque, and reluctance torque are evaluated. A prototype of 24-slot/14-pole double-six-phase 15°-shifting PMSM is manufactured, and the experiments are carried out to verify the performance of the machine.
